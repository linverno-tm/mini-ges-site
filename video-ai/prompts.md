## Shot 1: aerial, the intake
Slow drone descent over the Great Fergana Canal: a huge main irrigation canal about 35 m wide with sloped concrete banks
and service roads, carrying a massive fast flow through flat farmland lined with poplar trees. On one bank a much smaller side intake branches off at a gentle curve: water turns smoothly out of the canal into a concrete
headworks with two grey steel gates on red wheels under a yellow hoist frame. Behind the gates a straight concrete
channel runs away from the canal across the fields toward a small blue industrial building on the horizon.

## Shot 2: trash rack and gate
Close-up inside a deep concrete intake chamber: clear water flows through a tall, steeply inclined screen of dark
steel bars (a trash rack); a few leaves and twigs are caught on the bars. The camera tilts up and follows the water
under a heavy grey steel wheeled gate with red wheels that slowly lifts on chains, letting a strong, smooth flow pass.

## Shot 3: derivation channel
Low tracking shot just above the water racing down a long, straight, rectangular concrete channel with smooth walls,
poplar trees passing on both sides. The flow is fast and glassy. Ahead, the channel ends at a modern powerhouse:
a reinforced-concrete frame building clad in blue metal panels.

## Shot 4: turbines (cutaway)
Inside the powerhouse, a cutaway view: two large horizontal bulb turbine units side by side in a concrete pit, each
with a ribbed blue steel casing. The casing becomes transparent and reveals a four-blade propeller runner with broad
polished bronze-steel blades and a red hub, spinning fast as water rushes through the tube. A red overhead crane beam
spans the hall above.

## Shot 5: generator and switchyard
The turbine shaft drives a horizontal generator; in a cutaway the copper windings glow and faint blue electric arcs
flicker as power is produced. Glowing lines of energy travel out through cables to an outdoor switchyard beside the
building: tall galvanized round steel columns on concrete plinths, crossbeams, and three brown porcelain insulators
on a steel frame. Small sparks at the insulator tips.

## Shot 6: tailrace and the grid
Water leaves the powerhouse through two outlets, foaming white, and flows along a short curved concrete channel back
into the huge Great Fergana Canal, merging with its powerful current. The camera pulls back into a wide aerial at golden hour:
power lines run from the switchyard across the fields to a village where house lights switch on one by one.
